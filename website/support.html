Support

Requirements

PhotoSur requires Android 8.0 (API 26) or later, or iOS 15 or later. Automatic background removal runs on-device and may not work on very old or low-memory devices — the manual brush tool is always available as a fallback.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does automatic removal sometimes miss details?

On-device AI works well for most subjects but isn't perfect, especially around fine detail like hair or fur. Use the Manual brush tab to paint away or restore any missed areas.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. All processing happens on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I remove ads?

Go to Settings → Go Ad-Free inside the app. This is a one-time $14.99 purchase (not a subscription) that removes all ads — every feature stays free either way.

I paid for ad-free but I'm still seeing ads.

Go to Settings → Restore Purchases. If the issue persists after that, contact us below with your purchase receipt.

The app crashed / a feature isn't working.

Try force-closing and reopening the app first. If the problem continues, email us with your device model and OS version.
