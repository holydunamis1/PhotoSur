PhotoSur

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Your photos

We do not collect your photos. Images are processed entirely on your device — background removal, brush edits, and exports never leave your phone unless you choose to save or share them yourself.

Advertising

PhotoSur is ad-supported on the free tier. We use Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers for ad personalization. On iOS, we request your permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before any personalized tracking occurs — you can decline, and non-personalized ads will still be shown. See Google's own privacy policy for details on how AdMob handles data: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Optional HD cut-out model

If you choose to download the optional HD cut-out model, the app fetches one file from this app's public GitHub release page. GitHub sees the request (including your IP address) as it would for any download, as described in GitHub's own privacy statement. Your photos are never uploaded: the model runs on your phone. You can remove the downloaded file at any time in Settings.

Purchases

The optional one-time $14.99 ad-removal purchase is processed entirely through Apple's App Store or Google Play — we do not receive or store your payment information.

No account required

PhotoSur does not require an account, and we do not operate a backend server that stores your data. Export history and settings are stored locally on your device only.
